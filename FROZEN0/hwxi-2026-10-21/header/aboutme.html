About Me

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering and Computer Science at University of Cincinnati. Before joining UC, I was a member of PacSoft (Pacific Software Research Centre) working on the Formal Methods project. My supervisor was Jim Hook. I joined PacSoft in August 1998 after finishing my PhD thesis Dependent Types in Practical Programming under the supervision of Frank Pfenning and graduated from Carnegie Mellon University.
